Higher Education ERP and SIS Modernization Assessment

Western State University invites proposals from qualified higher education advisory firms to evaluate the institution's current ERP, SIS, and HCM platforms.

The selected firm will:
- assess current-state enterprise systems
- conduct stakeholder interviews
- evaluate future-state ERP and SIS options
- review implementation risk
- recommend sequencing, governance, and change management

Experience in higher education technology advisory is required.
