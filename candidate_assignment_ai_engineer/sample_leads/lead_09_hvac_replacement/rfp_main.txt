Invitation for Bids
HVAC Replacement and Plumbing Renovation

The City of Brookhaven invites licensed contractors to provide all labor, materials, equipment, demolition, installation, and commissioning for HVAC replacement and plumbing renovation at the public safety annex.

This is a construction and mechanical contracting project that includes:
- demolition of existing systems
- plumbing replacement
- HVAC installation
- ductwork
- equipment startup
- site supervision

No consulting services are requested.
