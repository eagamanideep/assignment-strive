Regional Workforce and Disparity Study

The Metro Workforce Board is requesting proposals from firms with expertise in workforce development, labor market analysis, economic inclusion, and disparity studies.

The resulting work should include:
- current-state workforce assessment
- labor market and employer demand analysis
- DBE/MBE/WBE disparity evaluation
- stakeholder outreach
- economic mobility recommendations
- implementation roadmap for supplier diversity improvement

Respondents should have prior experience with public sector workforce boards, economic impact analysis, and community needs assessments.
