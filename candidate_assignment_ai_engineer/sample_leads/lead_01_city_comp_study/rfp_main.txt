Request for Proposals
Citywide Classification and Compensation Study

The City of Lakemont is soliciting proposals from qualified firms to perform a citywide classification and compensation study covering all full-time and part-time municipal positions.

Scope of work includes:
- review of current job descriptions
- compensation benchmarking against peer jurisdictions
- pay equity assessment
- salary schedule recommendations
- reclassification recommendations
- stakeholder interviews with department leadership
- implementation roadmap for updated compensation bands

The selected consultant should demonstrate experience with public sector HR consulting, classification and compensation, salary surveys, and organizational analysis.
